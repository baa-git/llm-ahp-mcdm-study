# Appendix and table files

Generated files of the study report (`ahp-bench report ...`, see the repository README). Row counts exclude the header row. Full descriptions of the tables are in `../report.md`; the Turkish technical report is `../TEKNIK_RAPOR.md`. Conditions are shown with the short labels guided, direct and CoT plus the columns guidance (guided/unguided) and technique (zero-shot/CoT); in the raw records direct is stored under the key `lean` and CoT under `cot`.

## Appendix (`results/report/appendix/`)

| File | ID | Rows | Content |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `A04_group_matrices.csv` | A04 | 36 | Geometric-mean group matrices (experts; LLM panel per condition, first answers of tag 'main') with eigenvector weights, lambda_max, CI and CR. |
| `A05_model_matrices.csv` | A05 | 243 | Per-model geometric-mean matrices of the repetitions (model x condition x task) with weights, lambda_max, CI and CR. |
| `A06_group_matrices_after_revision.csv` | A06 | 18 | Geometric-mean LLM panel matrices after revision (tag 'revision_final': best valid answers of each chain; matrices that needed no revision unchanged), conditions Unguided, zero-shot (direct); Unguided, CoT. |
| `A07_run_level.csv` | A07 | 270 | One row per selected record of tag 'main': CR, eigenvector weights (w1..w5 in the criterion order of the task), provider, tokens, cost, parse flags, revision need, revision calls, revisions used, final CR and judge verdicts (tag 'revision_final'; empty for conditions outside the revision design). |
| `A08_judge_feedback.csv` | A08 | 250 | Judge feedback text and verdict for every revision call of tag 'revision_final' (CR before = CR of the best answers the revision was built from; CR after empty when the reply was unparsable). |
| `cot_reasoning.csv` | A03 | 90 | Ranking and reasoning text returned by each model in the CoT condition. |
| `matrices_expert.csv` | A02 | 287 | Long-format list of every expert judgement matrix entry with the matrix CR. |
| `matrices_llm.csv` | A01 | 5535 | Long-format list of every selected LLM judgement matrix entry with the matrix CR. |

## Tables (`results/report/tables/`)

| File | Rows | Content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `T01_models_settings.csv` | 9 | Models and settings: Lists each model with its developer, OpenRouter identifier, requested temperature, applied reasoning setting, mean reasoning tokens, providers seen, number of calls, number of parse failures and total cost (USD) for the selected run tag. |
| `T02_expert_consistency.csv` | 9 | Expert consistency: Gives the consistency ratio (CR) of each expert's main-criteria and delivery sub-criteria matrix, the CR of the geometric-mean group matrices, and the number of matrices with CR ≤ 0.10. |
| `T03_llm_consistency.csv` | 30 | LLM consistency: Gives, per model and condition, the mean and maximum CR over repetitions and the number of matrices with CR ≤ 0.10 for the main and delivery tasks; panel rows pool all models and add the CR of the geometric-mean group matrix. |
| `T04_main_weights.csv` | 5 | Main-criteria weights: Lists the expert group weights and each condition's LLM panel group weights for the five main criteria, with the LLM minus expert difference in percentage points. |
| `T05_delivery_local_weights.csv` | 4 | Delivery local weights: Lists the expert group weights and each condition's LLM panel group weights for the four delivery sub-criteria (local weights), with the LLM minus expert difference in percentage points. |
| `T06_global_weights.csv` | 8 | Global weights: Lists the expert group weights and each condition's LLM panel group weights for the eight global indicators, with the LLM minus expert difference in percentage points. |
| `T07_model_weights_main.csv` | 27 | Model-level main weights: Gives, per model and condition, the main-criteria weights from the geometric mean of the model's repetition matrices, the number of repetitions used and the top-ranked criterion. |
| `T08_similarity_to_experts.csv` | 30 | Similarity to expert weights: Gives Spearman rho, Kendall tau, RMSE and mean absolute difference (percentage points) between each model's (and each condition panel's) group weights and the expert group weights, for the five main and the eight global weights. |
| `T09_run_to_run_stability.csv` | 27 | Run-to-run stability: Gives, per model and condition, the sample standard deviation and coefficient of variation of each main-criteria weight across repetitions, their mean CV, and the number of distinct top-ranked criteria across repetitions. |
| `T10_one_per_developer.csv` | 9 | Panel composition variants: Gives, per condition, the panel main-criteria group weights and their similarity to the expert weights (main and global) for all models, one model per developer (claude-haiku-4.5 and gpt-5.2 removed) and the panel without gpt-5.2. |
| `T11_supplier_rankings.csv` | 20 | Supplier rankings (rates): Gives TOPSIS, ARAS and EDAS scores and ranks of the suppliers under the expert global weights and each condition's LLM panel global weights, with delivery indicators as rates. |
| `T12_rankings_counts_vs_rates.csv` | 10 | Rankings: counts vs rates: Gives supplier ranks from TOPSIS, ARAS and EDAS with delivery indicators as counts and as rates, under the expert global weights and the LLM panel global weights of the Unguided, zero-shot (direct) condition. |
| `T13_equal_weight_baseline.csv` | 5 | Equal-weight baseline: Gives TOPSIS, ARAS and EDAS scores and ranks of the suppliers with all eight global weights equal to 1/8 and delivery indicators as rates. |
| `T14_weight_sensitivity.csv` | 71 | Main-criterion weight sensitivity: For each of the five main criteria in turn, sets its main weight to each level from 0.05 to 0.70 in steps of 0.05 while the other four main criteria share the remainder equally; the Delivery main weight is split equally over D1-D4, giving eight global weights. Gives the global weights and, for TOPSIS, ARAS (eta = 1) and EDAS with delivery indicators as rates, the supplier ranking (initials, best first: A = Alpha_Inc, B = Beta_Supplies, D = Delta_Logistics, E = Epsilon_Group, G = Gamma_Co) and the top supplier. The first row is the equal-weight reference (all eight global weights 1/8). |
| `T15_sensitivity_summary.csv` | 15 | Weight sensitivity summary: Summarises T14 per varied main criterion and method: the top supplier at the lowest (0.05) and highest (0.70) level, the number of distinct supplier rankings over the 14 levels, the number of levels whose top supplier equals the equal-weight top supplier, and the lowest level at which the top supplier differs from the equal-weight top supplier (empty if never). |
| `T16_revision_summary.csv` | 40 | Revision summary: Gives, per model, condition and task (revision run tag 'revision_final', source tag 'main'), the number of source matrices, the number with CR > 0.10 (needing revision), the cumulative number that reached CR ≤ 0.10 after at most 1 to 5 revisions, the number not reached, the number of chains with no revision record yet, and the mean initial and mean final CR of the matrices needing revision (final = the best valid answers of the chain, i.e. the lowest CR among the first answers and all parsable revisions); CR ≤ 0.10 means CR rounded to 4 decimals is at most 0.10; panel rows pool all models. |
| `T17_weights_before_after_revision.csv` | 5 | Weights before and after revision: Gives, per condition, the LLM panel group weights (main criteria, delivery local and global) computed from the first answers and from the final answers after revision (the best valid answers of each chain; matrices that needed no revision are used unchanged), next to the expert group weights, with Spearman rho and RMSE (percentage points) against the expert main and global weights. |
| `T18_temperature_comparison.csv` | 3 | Temperature comparison (Unguided, zero-shot (direct)): Gives, for the Unguided, zero-shot (direct) condition, per run tag and requested temperature (the main tag at its recorded temperature, temp_<value> tags at <value>), the panel main-criteria group weights, the number of main and delivery matrices with CR ≤ 0.10, and the mean over models of each model's mean coefficient of variation of the main weights across repetitions. |
| `T20_logic_preservation.csv` | 35 | Logic preservation after revision: Gives, per model, condition and task (revision run tag 'revision_final', source tag 'main') and pooled panel rows, for the chains with at least one revision record: the number of chains, the number whose final answers reached CR ≤ 0.10, the mean number of revision calls used (until the first consistent result, else all calls), and, comparing the final answers (the best valid answers: lowest CR among the first answers and all parsable revisions) with the chain's first answers, the mean number of changed answers, the mean Spearman rho of the eigenvector weights, the share with the same top criterion, the share with the same full ranking and the mean of the largest absolute weight change (percentage points). The last three columns count the judge verdicts (logic preserved yes/partly/no) on the revision that gave the final answers (none when the first answers stayed best); they are empty when no judge data exist. |
| `T21_ranking_agreement.csv` | 12 | Ranking agreement with the expert weights: Gives, for TOPSIS, ARAS and EDAS with delivery indicators as rates, Spearman rho and Kendall tau between the supplier ranks under each condition's LLM panel global weights (and under equal weights) and the supplier ranks under the expert global weights, and the number of suppliers whose rank differs; ranks as in T11 and T13. |
| `T22_guided_compliance.csv` | 26 | Replies and the order stated in the guided prompt: Shows whether the replies followed the order of the criteria stated in the guided prompt, read from the prompt file - main: the numbered priority order Quality > Compliance > Delivery > Financial > Volume (instructions.priority_guidance); delivery: only the first criterion, D1 Delivered, named the primary positive indicator (instructions.example_for_delivery; no full order, so the order columns are empty). For each set of replies (selected first answers of the run tag): the number of replies, the number whose eigenvector-weight ranking equals the prompted order, the number with the prompted first criterion ranked first, and the mean Spearman rho between the weights and the prompted order. Rows marked 'reference' are replies that never saw this order (direct and CoT conditions) and the experts' individual matrices. |
